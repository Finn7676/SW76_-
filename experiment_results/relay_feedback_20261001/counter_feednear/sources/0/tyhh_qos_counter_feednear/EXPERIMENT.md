# H2 counter-feednear

Parent is `tyhh_qos_relay_counter`. After round 400 this version allows a
donor of length up to 12 to donate to an adjacent friendly body with observed
recipient length at least equal to the donor; equal lengths use a deterministic
dragon-ID tie break. The population reserve is three and the local enemy-free
gate remains. Portal and combat logic are unchanged. Results are accepted only
after the fixed 40-game replay audit.
